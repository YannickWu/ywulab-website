Join Us

We are always seeking talented and passionate scientists and engineers to join our team, from students to postdocs. Our research are highly multidisciplinary, and collaborative efforts are essential to our progress.

Opportunities

Postdoctoral Fellows

We welcome applications from highly motivated researchers interested in interdisciplinary work spanning materials science, physics, electronics, and biomedical engineering.

What we are looking for

  • A Ph.D. in materials science and engineering, bioengineering, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, chemical engineering, biology, chemistry, and physics.
  • Strong experimental and analytical skills.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively.

Interested applicants should send a CV, a brief description of research interests, and representative publications with the subject line “Postdoctoral Application.”

To appreciate the effort you putting into the application, Dr. Wu will read and reply to every received email with thoughtful consideration.

Graduate Students

Applicants from physics, materials science, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, biomedical engineering, and related backgrounds are encouraged to contact us.

Please send a CV, transcripts, and a brief description of your research interests with the subject line “Graduate Student Application.”

To appreciate the effort you putting into the application, Dr. Wu will read and reply to every received email with thoughtful consideration.

Undergraduate Students

Undergraduate students interested in gaining hands-on research experience are encouraged to reach out.

Research opportunities may involve materials fabrication, electronic devices, sensing systems, data analysis, and interdisciplinary projects.
